Generated by `internal/raft/bench_test.go`. Raw per-trial data is in `bench-results/`.

**Network:** node-to-node RPC is in-memory (goroutines and Go channels via `NetworkRegistry`), not a real network. These numbers measure the algorithm and its timers in a single process, not network latency, serialization or disk I/O.

**Build:** run WITHOUT `-race`.

## Leader election (ms)

Cold start: fresh cluster per trial, measured from just before `startCluster` (which arms each node's election timer in `NewNode`) until the first node reports `Leader`. Re-election: fresh cluster, leader elected, 200ms of heartbeats, `Kill()` the leader, measured until a survivor is `Leader` in a higher term. Both polled every 1ms. Timeout 5s; timed-out trials are counted as failures, and the percentiles cover successful trials only.
